Authenticity & Registry

Boutique Policies & Quality Guarantees

Every bridal lehenga, groom sherwani, and heritage saree available at Zari Ethnics is direct-sourced from master artisans in Varanasi, Jaipur, Lucknow, and Kolkata. We guarantee original zari threadwork and do not stock mass-produced machine imitations.
Alteration services are complimentary for all garments purchased at full retail price. Bespoke creations (sherwanis and lehengas tailored to specific measurements) require an on-site master session to verify metrics prior to starting production.
To protect physical registry exclusivities, custom wedding attire is non-exchangeable once tailoring is initiated. Unaltered standard retail items may be exchanged within 7 days of acquisition, supported by original showroom invoices.
