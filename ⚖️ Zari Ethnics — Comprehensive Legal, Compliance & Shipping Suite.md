🛒 1. Shopping Cart & Digital Checkout System

The Zari Ethnics website uses a zero-database, serverless shopping bag framework designed to eliminate online registry storage costs while keeping customer transaction data secure:

[Add to Bag] ➔ Stored in localStorage ➔ Encoded to Invoice ➔ Handed off to WhatsApp Secure API


Local Processing: When you add standard retail items, bespoke groom sherwanis, or luxury jewelry to your bag, the selection is compiled locally in your browser's secure localStorage cache.

Serverless Environment: No banking details, passwords, or personal identifying profiles are archived on our web hosting servers, protecting you against data breaches.

4.1 Standard Retail Goods (CPA Section 20)

For standard, unaltered stock items (such as standard-sized kurtas, accessories, or jewelry) purchased at our standard retail price:

You are entitled to exchange the item or request a boutique credit within 7 business days of purchase or courier receipt.

Prerequisites: The garment must be completely unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and remain in its original pristine packaging with all original boutique security tags intact. The original showroom receipt must be presented.

4.2 Custom-Made / Bespoke Attire Exclusion (CPA Section 20(3)(a))

The Law: Under Section 20(3)(a) of the CPA, the standard consumer right of return does not apply to transactions where goods have been custom-made, altered, personalized, or tailored to a customer's specific measurements.

Application: Once raw fabric cutting, hand-weaving, or custom zardozi embroidery has commenced on a custom bridal lehenga or groom's sherwani, the order is strictly non-refundable and non-exchangeable.

Final Sign-Off: Customers who opt for in-showroom collection must physically fit and sign off on their garment before taking delivery. Zari Ethnics accepts no liability for fit issues arising from subsequent personal weight fluctuations once the garment leaves our doors.

4.3 Deposit and Cancellation Policy

Deposits paid for custom bridal or groom commissions secure the raw materials and block artisan slots in South Asia.

Cancellations within 48 Hours: 100% refundable.

Cancellations after 48 Hours: 50% cancellation fee applies to cover raw silk, dye preparation, and initial designer booking costs.

Cancellations after Weaving Begins: 100% forfeiture of deposit.
