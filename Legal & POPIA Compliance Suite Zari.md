1. POPIA Privacy Policy
1.1 Introduction
Zari Ethnics respect your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). This Privacy Policy explains how we process the information you provide when using our website and digital concierge services.
1.2 Information We Collect
We collect personal information directly from you when you interact with our website, book a private appointment, or inquire about products. This information may include:
* Identity Information: Full Name and Surname.
* Contact Information: Mobile/WhatsApp Number, Email Address.
* Garment Sizing Data: Sizing, measurement metrics, and styling preferences.
* Inquiry Details: Custom messages regarding fabric, styling, or wedding attire requirements.

Your personal information is processed strictly for boutique operations, including:
1. Facilitating WhatsApp-based catalogue inquiries and generating custom pricing estimates.
2. Scheduling fitting and tailoring appointments at our Trade Route Mall showroom.
3. Managing custom measurements for custom-tailored sherwanis and lehengas.
4. Answering customer support or product availability requests.
Note: Our website utilizes direct-to-WhatsApp link generation. This means that when you click "Inquire on WhatsApp" or "Generate Booking", your data is processed directly through your device's secure WhatsApp client and is not archived or stored on our web hosting servers.
1.4 Consent and Security
By completing an inquiry or booking form on this website, you explicitly consent to Zari Ethnics processing your personal data for the intended purpose. We implement industry-standard security measures to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of any offline client registries.
1.5 Your Rights under POPIA
You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or object to the processing of your data. To exercise these rights, please contact our Information Officer at zariethnics@gmail.com.
2. Refund, Return, and Exchange Policy
2.1 Standard Retail Purchases
Under the South African Consumer Protection Act (CPA), standard, unaltered retail items purchased directly from our showroom may be exchanged within 7 days of purchase, provided that:
* The garment is completely unworn, undamaged, unaltered, and in its original pristine condition.
* All original designer tags, labels, and packaging remain intact.
* The original Zari Ethnics showroom invoice/receipt is presented.
2.2 Custom-Tailored and Bespoke Garments (Crucial CPA Exclusion)
In accordance with Section 20(3) (a) of the South African Consumer Protection Act, the standard right of return and refund does not apply to goods that have been custom-made, custom-sized, or personalized to the consumer's specifications.
* Bespoke Attire: All custom bridal lehengas, customized designer groom sherwanis, and garments altered by our on-site master tailors are strictly non-refundable and non-exchangeable once tailoring or raw material preparation has commenced.
* Fitting Sign-Off: Customers are required to attend on-site verification and fitting sessions at our Trade Route Mall showroom. Once a custom garment is signed off and collected, Zari Ethnics accepts no liability for sizing changes due to subsequent weight fluctuations.
2.3 Order Cancellations
Deposits paid toward custom-tailored bespoke bridal or groom collections are strictly non-refundable once fabric cutting or specialized hand-embroidery work has begun.
